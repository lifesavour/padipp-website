Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Padipp is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Padipp, we collect the following information:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google.
  • Quiz Performance Data: We store your quiz attempts, scores, accuracy percentages, and subject-wise performance to provide personalized insights.
  • Device Information: Basic device information required for the app to function correctly.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your Padipp account
  • Display your performance statistics and progress
  • Show weekly leaderboards and rankings
  • Identify your weak subjects and personalize your learning experience
  • Improve the app and add new features
  • Communicate with you about app updates or support

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

Padipp does not collect:

  • Your precise location or GPS data
  • Your phone contacts or call logs
  • Your camera, microphone, or media files
  • Any financial or payment information
  • Your age, gender, or other sensitive personal attributes

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on our servers hosted on Railway (railway.app). We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Your Google authentication is handled entirely by Google's secure OAuth 2.0 system and Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your Google password.

5. Third-Party Services

Padipp uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication: For secure user authentication. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Railway.app: For hosting our backend servers and database.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With your consent
  • To comply with legal obligations or court orders
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Padipp, our users, or the public

7. Leaderboard and Public Information

Your display name and weekly quiz score are visible to other users on the Padipp leaderboard. If you do not wish to appear on the leaderboard, you may contact us to request removal.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account information and quiz history for as long as your account is active. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please contact us at support@padipp.app and we will process your request within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Padipp is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Padipp after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:

  • Email: support@padipp.app
  • Website: padipp.app
